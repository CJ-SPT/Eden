## Capsule destruction and home-station respawn, November 7, 2026

Pilot turrets, missiles and light/medium combat drones now accept capsule targets through the same targeting, range, authoritative presence and Crimewatch boundaries as other pilot ships. Capsule recognition uses catalog group 29 for ordinary and variant capsules. Unprovoked lowsec capsule aggression requires disabled safety and records criminal rather than suspect aggression; existing lawful flag/outlaw/engagement rights still apply. Unsupported highsec criminal aggression remains blocked. NPCs continue to ignore capsules.

The shared `PilotLossSettlement` delegates ordinary hull loss to the existing capsule-escape/wreck/insurance path. Capsule loss instead creates a fresh standard capsule docked at the saved home station, records the destroyed capsule and home transfer, and removes the old capsule and active physical clone body in the same damage transaction. Installed implants are destroyed and included as destroyed items in the victim/attacker combat reports. Wallet, trained skills, stored ships/items, other installed clone bodies, jump-clone cooldown and character-owned cerebral accelerators remain intact. Training advances with the saved old rate up to the impact timestamp, then rebases the remaining queue after implant removal. There is no capsule insurance payment, NPC bounty/security/standing reward or ordinary ship wreck. Persistent recoverable corpse objects remain separate work.

Current and timed-offline pilots use that same settlement. A dead offline capsule's presence row is removed, so its docked replacement never appears as an in-space contact. Existing home-station fallback rules are reused without changing saved home settings or charging a transfer fee. If neither home nor fallback can resolve, or any loss/clone write fails, the full damage/payment/timer/body/history transaction rolls back. Concurrent callbacks settle one loss, one respawn and one set of participant reports. The existing schema-100 loss, clone, implant and training records suffice; no save migration is added.

The victim receives native incoming damage, the argument-free `OnPlayerPodDeath` notification, terminal old-pod removal and then the station/ship/system session delta. No replacement ego is added to the source ballpark. The retained flight session adopts the destination's static objects, clears old movement/travel/scene state and remains docked, including when home is in another system. Named inventory changes prime the removed and replacement capsules before the session delta; implant, character-attribute, training/brain and clone-cache updates follow the existing adapters. Tests assert emitted ordering and values; they do not establish native animation or input acceptance.

## Offline missile contact reads, November 7, 2026

A missile-impact transaction previously called the general presence fallback for a logged-off victim. That fallback rebuilt a pilot snapshot in a second write transaction, which locked against the missile transaction. The previous verified build reproduces the same failure.

`IPilotCombatPresence.FindOnline` supplies the existing in-memory contact without an offline database fallback. Missile operations prefer that live contact and otherwise use the active pilot, position and unexpired logoff row already read in their own world transaction. They recheck the same ship/system identity and preserve zero offline velocity. Automatic retaliation loads/validates candidates before contact lookup. No observer packet or damage/timer formula changes. The general `Find` operation retains its existing live/offline observer behavior.

## Pilot missile combat and automatic retaliation (November 6, 2026)

Supported missile launchers now admit locked pilot-owned ships. Each paid cycle uses the existing safety/legality checks, ammunition, cooldown, flight drive and warhead. Grouped launchers either pay every member or roll back every member. The weapons/PvP and applicable suspect/criminal timers begin at launch; changing safety later does not revoke a paid projectile. Criminal highsec launches remain rejected pending CONCORD. Capsules are excluded by their catalog group.

Missiles follow authoritative pilot motion and use the target's current fitted signature, speed and defenses at impact. Pilot-target launches retain their exact FILETIME rather than rounding birth down to a second; moving interception therefore starts from the same instant as its source/target geometry. Existing Carbon flight integration, intercept steering, swept contact, explosion application and orphan behavior are reused. Warp, docking, system departure, expired offline presence or hull replacement remove the target; an orphan never reacquires it. Source departure, disconnect, loss or cancellation retain the existing outgoing-projectile cancellation policy. Competing observers share saved flight boundaries, and impact settlement rechecks source/target hull identity after earlier fatal steps.

Impact health, contributions, PvP deadline updates, capsule escape, wrecks, insurance and participant reports commit together with the projectile's terminal state and saved attacker report. Failed impact settlement rolls back the flight and loss while retaining the ammunition/cooldown already paid at launch. Capsuleer impacts pay no NPC bounty or pirate security reward. Offline victims remain hittable through their saved presence deadline; applied damage extends their PvP deadline. Startup expires flying projectiles without replay or refund and preserves completed impact reports for exactly-once delivery.

Automatic missiles choose the nearest eligible NPC or pilot aggressor without a target lock, using the existing center-distance and identity tie-break rules. Schema **92** adds indexed directional `WorldPilotHostility` records to the shared paid player-aggression operation, covering tackle, turrets and missile launches. Only a ship that actually aggressed this source is a pilot candidate; target locks, suspect flags and third-party attacks alone do not qualify it. Capsule, unavailable and safety-rejected candidates are filtered before selection. The acquisition memory lasts through the paid cycle plus the existing 15-minute PvP interval, and expired rows are pruned on subsequent aggression. This bounded interval is explicit Eden acquisition policy, not a recovered private server AI rule. Historical timers cannot reconstruct who initiated old fights, so migration adds no guessed hostility records.

Schema 92 also adds `WorldMissile.TargetKind`, preserving projectile identities, payloads, paid deadlines and the allocation sequence. Existing pilot-sourced missiles default to NPC targets; NPC-sourced missiles are classified as pilot targets. The idempotent upgrade handles current saves and older missile-schema rebuilds. The narrow `IPilotCombatPresence` gameplay interface supplies authoritative live/offline contacts without exposing protocol objects. Named incoming damage events now carry weapon-independent health/damage fields shared by turrets and missiles. Victim updates are consumed immediately after missile advancement, before repair or NPC work can run against a destroyed hull; existing before-flight damage messages and capsule transitions are retained.

## Pilot turret combat and loss settlement (November 6, 2026)

Supported civilian and ammunition-fed turrets can now attack another pilot's locked, nearby active ship. Grouped turrets share the existing ammunition, crystal wear, capacitor, cooldown, accuracy and damage calculations. The target's fitted defenses, signature and authoritative space velocity feed application. Each repeat rechecks availability, range and Crimewatch legality before spending resources. Full safety rejects unlawful empire attacks; lowsec aggression with partial/disabled safety flags the attacker, while flagged targets and limited engagements permit lawful retaliation. Nullsec attacks need no suspect/criminal flag. Criminal highsec activation remains blocked pending CONCORD. Misses still pay the cycle and aggression timers.

Damage and fatal settlement run in one world transaction, including every bank member, both pilots' aggression deadlines and contribution records. A failure after a member pays or during loss creation rolls everything back. Fatal hits use the same loss owner as NPC damage: destroy the hull, preserve dropped/destroyed inventory manifests, create the catalog wreck, settle eligible insurance once and place the victim in a capsule. The victim and participating capsuleers receive saved combat reports with character, hull and weapon identities. Capsuleer kills create no NPC bounty or pirate security reward. Wreck ownership remains with the victim under the existing owner-only access policy; ordinary theft/loot rights are still pending.

Schema **91** makes the historical loss row's NPC attacker nullable and adds `AttackerPilotId`. Existing NPC losses, manifests, capsule identities and wreck references survive the atomic table replacement. The migration verifies all foreign keys before clearing SQLite's deferred reference to the replaced table, and reopening is idempotent. Historical interaction backfill continues to interpret only NPC-era losses; current participant reports are retained. No assets, skills or currency grants are introduced.

Connected victims receive named incoming damage messages, health attributes, insurance updates and the existing capsule transition. Incoming player damage uses `(UE_OWNERID=2, characterID)` for `owner`, with the attacking ship as `source`/`attackerID`; grouped hits set `isBanked`. Pending loss transitions are consumed before movement checkpoints, RPC dispatch or disconnect cleanup, preventing a stale session from republishing its destroyed ship. Shared-space health updates reach observers; a destroyed contact is removed immediately. Offline victims retain the extended PvP deadline and switch their saved presence to the new capsule. Reconnecting reads the persisted active hull. No offline shots are simulated.

This is the turret portion of the combat roadmap. Capsule destruction and clone consequences, missile/drone pilot targets, security-status penalties, sentries/CONCORD, corporation/war/duel rights and expanded loot/assistance rights remain later milestones. Turret pilot combat uses the transactional path; warmed NPC turret combat retains the existing resident optimization.

## Enforced safety and persistent aggression (November 6, 2026)

Existing player tackle now checks typed aggression rules before spending capacitor or saving a cycle. Full safety blocks unlawful player aggression in empire space; partial permits suspect actions; disabled also permits lowsec capsule aggression with a criminal flag. Nullsec aggression needs no suspect/criminal flag. Flagged targets, outlaws and active limited engagements permit lawful retaliation. Alpha pilots have effective full safety in highsec. Criminal highsec activation is explicitly rejected even with disabled safety until CONCORD enforcement is implemented. This is a player-tackle foundation: turret, missile and drone damage still targets NPCs; corporation/war/duel rights, player damage/loss attribution, security penalties, CONCORD/sentries and loot/assistance rights remain future work.

World schema **90** saves weapons, PvP, NPC, suspect and criminal timer deadlines, bilateral limited engagements and offline ship-presence deadlines. Paid activity remains active through its cycle, followed by 60 seconds for weapons, 15 minutes for PvP and suspect/criminal flags, five minutes for NPC aggression and five minutes for engagements. The victim receives PvP aggression without an attacker weapons flag. Criminal precedence survives subsequent suspect actions. NPC guns, missiles, drones, tackle and applied electronic warfare record NPC aggression; resident gunfire updates its in-memory timer and projects it with the paid shot at checkpoint. Rejected actions leave capacitor, cooldowns, effects and timers unchanged.

Weapons timers prevent docking and gate jumps. Criminal timers prevent the admitted travel actions in highsec and gate entry into highsec. Ordinary lowsec/nullsec warp remains available subject to tackle. Typed rules also expose ship-change and safe-logoff checks; native in-space boarding and a safe-logoff countdown are not implemented by this milestone. Existing ship changes remain station-only.

Disconnect and character Log Off keep an in-space ship visible at its saved position for 60 seconds, five minutes with an NPC timer, or 15 minutes with a PvP timer. Outgoing effects stop; incoming player tackle remains and another paid hostile cycle extends the offline victim's saved deadline. Expired bodies disappear and reject further tackle. Reconnect removes the offline record without duplicating the ship or clearing aggression. Startup reconciles saved combat deadlines after a crash without renewing them on repeated restarts. This retained body is stationary; emergency warp and world-owned offline NPC combat remain future work. Downtime never replays damage.

`crimewatch.GetClientStates()` now returns `(timers, engagements, (criminals, suspects), safety)`. The timer tuple order is weapons, PvP, NPC, criminal/suspect, disapproval; each entry is `(state, expiry)`, using `None` while active/idle and absolute FILETIME during countdown. The engagement dictionary maps pilot IDs to `-1` while ongoing or to its expiry. Scoped monitoring sends `OnWeaponsTimerUpdate`, `OnPvpTimerUpdate`, `OnNpcTimerUpdate`, `OnCriminalTimerUpdate`, `OnSystemCriminalFlagUpdates` and the four engagement lifecycle events only on changes. `OnCrimewatchEngagementStopTimeout` takes just the other pilot ID. The client applies a successful safety selection itself, so the server does not duplicate its safety event.

## Combat calculation groundwork, November 2, 2026

A pure calculation layer now combines turret range and angular tracking penalties, evaluates shot quality from an explicit random draw, and applies the four damage types through shield, armor and structure. Each layer consumes only the raw damage needed to exhaust it before the remaining original damage mixture meets the next layer's own resonances. An intact immune layer stops damage; an empty immune layer does not. Damage and health inputs reject negative or nonfinite values.

The accuracy model is `0.5^(trackingPenalty² + rangePenalty²)`, with `trackingPenalty = angularVelocity * signatureReference / (tracking * targetSignature)` and `rangePenalty = max(0, distance - optimal) / falloff`. Zero tracking or falloff cannot produce NaN at their boundaries. The [CCP accuracy discussion](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/upcoming-balance-changes) describes signature scaling and 50% accuracy at one falloff or matching angular/tracking velocity. It is historical context, not proof of the current server implementation. Eden's initial shot-quality model uses the same draw for hit selection and quality: a successful draw below 0.01 does triple damage, otherwise the multiplier is draw + 0.49. This RNG policy has not been verified against Tranquility's server.

## Drone NPC damage messages and frame diagnostics (November 8, 2026)

A maintainer reported missing incoming damage messages in a rogue-drone combat site and choppy camera/UI
rendering without slow pilot-update warnings. They also noted concurrent work on main as a possible cause.
Investigation used an isolated worktree based on committed `cd817d3`, without the pending thermal changes.
The rendering slowdown has not been reproduced or attributed by these automated checks.

The notification adapter did contain an independent pinned-client incompatibility. NPCs with no
`gfxTurretID`, including Silverfish Alvi and several Strain and Sentient drone variants, sent `weapon=None`
with `attackType=otherPlayer`. The pinned logger's `AddCombatMessage` tests for key presence and calls
`evetypes.GetName` even when the value is None. Its `AttackMissRD` localization also requires a valid weapon
item. Omitting the weapon alone would therefore leave misses broken. The adapter now omits absent weapons
and selects `otherPlayerWeapons`, whose `AttackHitsR`/`AttackMissR` templates identify the source without
requiring a separate weapon. Hits, misses, source identity and damage attributes remain explicit. NPCs
with a graphics weapon retain their existing `otherPlayer` representation.
