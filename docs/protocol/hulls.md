## Tech I hull expansion milestone 1

The accepted goal covers all 275 published ship-category types whose build-3662168 client type definition has techLevel 1. Faction and special editions remain included even in specialist groups. The reproducible inventory is embedded as TechOneHulls.json with type IDs, groups, acquisition routes and effect IDs. There are 272 market routes and three recovery/developer-fixture routes. Export-HullCatalog.py reads the installed catalog in read-only mode and also exports the minimal first-cohort definitions and prerequisite skills. It includes no world/save data.

The first cohort adds Merlin (603), Punisher (597), Incursus (594) and Rifter (587) to Venture assembly. Gameplay owns the typed assembly admission rule. The existing station transaction retains single-item identity, shared allocation for stack splitting, all-or-nothing batch validation and idempotent assembled-ID retries. Boarding still validates owner, station, active-ship expectation and earned catalog skills. No world schema change is needed.

Hull fitted effects enter the existing exempt dogma graph. The hull's required skills supply catalog pre-multiplication of its racial bonus source attributes, including a zero-level source. Existing weapon and repair operations consume those evaluated module attributes. This gives Merlin small-hybrid damage and shield resistance, Punisher small-energy capacitor and armor resistance, Incursus small-hybrid damage and local armor repair amount, and Rifter small-projectile falloff and rate of fire. Required-skill selectors keep other weapon sizes/families separate. Existing corvette and Venture bonus owners retain their behavior.

Eligibility uses the type's publication and Tech I metadata together with a typed hull class. The usual tech level of a group does not exclude its qualifying special edition.

`HullBonusRules` owns the capability classification in Gameplay. Server loads every admitted hull effect and applies supported selectors to the existing fitting graph.
