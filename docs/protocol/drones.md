## Drone combat against pilot ships, November 7, 2026

The existing 41 light/medium combat-drone profiles on Brutix and Brutix Navy Issue now accept locked pilot ships as well as NPCs. Manual engagement checks current ownership/controller, system, authoritative target presence, lock and control range before changing any selected drone. Capsules, destroyed controllers, missing/expired contacts and rejected safety checks leave the entire selection and aggression state unchanged. Ongoing pursuit retains the existing lock/control-range policy, but cancels attacks when the target docks, warps, changes systems, disappears, dies or becomes illegal under current safety/engagement rights. Capsule destruction remains unsupported.

Accepted pilot attack orders begin shared Crimewatch aggression immediately. Each paid drone cycle rechecks legality and extends weapons/PvP, applicable flags, limited engagements and logout presence. Aggressive idle drones can defend against a new actual pilot aggressor within control range; passive mode waits for orders. Focus fire shares the existing stable candidate order with NPC aggressors. Schema **100** adds `WorldPilotHostility.LastAttackAt`, recorded by the shared offensive-action primitive. Existing rows retain expiry and receive timestamp zero, so migration cannot fabricate a new attack after the owner's last explicit drone order.

Drone shots use the target's fitted resistances, capacities, signature and authoritative velocity with the shared tracking/damage rules. Pilot hits publish the existing incoming damage signal and native health updates; outgoing combat text and drone effects retain their existing carriers. Destruction uses the same atomic capsule escape, wreck, insurance and participant-history settlement as pilot turrets/missiles. Several drones or concurrent callbacks cannot duplicate one loss. Pilot kills produce no NPC bounties, pirate standings or NPC security rewards.

Ordinary motion, nonfatal damage, contribution history and aggression updates run on resident immutable state under the world owner. Warmed owner/controller, observer and Crimewatch reads do not need SQL, connection opens or fitting calculations. Pending timers, hostility, engagements, health and drone deadlines are captured with the same detached checkpoint generation. SQL mutation boundaries project them before use; failed lethal settlement and failed detached projection preserve the previous paid state for retry. Restart retains damage/deadlines and releases control without replaying offline combat.

### Rules, combat and lifetime

Launch requires real owned bay contents, an in-space living Brutix, the drone's trained prerequisites, enough effective ship bandwidth and at most **min(Drones level, 5)** controlled drones. Selected quantities split into durable singleton identities; the final selected unit may retain its original stack identity. Repeated/concurrent requests cannot duplicate items or exceed limits. Recall returns those same individual IDs rather than creating replacement stacks. Insufficient bay capacity leaves the drone in space. Losing skills or effective bandwidth releases excess drones without moving or deleting them.

Light/Medium Drone Operation supplies 5% damage per level for its size; Drone Interfacing supplies 10%. A racial specialization supplies 2% only when that drone requires it. Drone Sharpshooting modifies optimal, Drone Navigation modifies chase speed and Drone Durability modifies all three HP capacities by their pinned 5%-per-level bonuses. Control range is 20 km plus 5 km per Drone Avionics level and 3 km per Advanced Drone Avionics level. Manual attack requires a locked live NPC and control-range validation; warping NPCs reject new orders and cancel existing drone attacks; accepted drones then maintain their own chase/orbit and firing intervals. Return-and-orbit cancels that attack. Passive drones wait for orders; aggressive drones may defend against NPCs that fire on their controlling ship after the last explicit order. Focus fire chooses a common attacker; disabled focus fire permits distributing drones across attackers. These target-selection policies are Eden's initial behavior, not a claim about Tranquility's private AI.

Drone motion reuses the existing Carbon-derived `OrbitCourse` and the free-ball mass/agility time constant. It distinguishes chase and orbit speeds in both authoritative movement and client orders. Reference: Carbon Destiny **27ee40b38a563d29054b32f64c3ebc87fabaec1f**, [Ballpark.cpp EvolveOldStyleOrbit](https://github.com/carbonengine/destiny/blob/27ee40b38a563d29054b32f64c3ebc87fabaec1f/src/Ballpark.cpp) and [Thunkers.cpp WriteBallToStream](https://github.com/carbonengine/destiny/blob/27ee40b38a563d29054b32f64c3ebc87fabaec1f/src/Thunkers.cpp). Free-ball Follow/Orbit snapshots retain follow ID/range and explicit mass, velocity, agility and speed fraction. See the [Carbon license](../carbon-license.md).

Turret-style drone shots use catalog tracking, signature and range with Eden's existing accuracy/quality and layered resistance calculations. Saved per-drone deadlines prevent duplicate cycles. NPC damage, hostility, kill history, faction/security changes, bounty/tax and wreck creation share the existing settlement transaction. Competing drones cannot settle a kill twice. Native combat messages and timed effects reach the owner and observers. NPC turrets can retaliate against attacking drones; an initial 25% target-selection opportunity shares the NPC's existing weapon deadline, with real shield/armor/structure damage and permanent drone loss. NPC missiles still target the controlling ship. The resident NPC fast path falls back to the same transactional route while drones are controlled, preventing it from bypassing retaliation.

Schema **85** adds `WorldDrone`, `WorldDroneSettings` and the last NPC weapon target, without granting drones, skills, ISK or health. Position, individual damage, paid deadlines and preferences persist. Ordinary one-second owner updates advance movement/combat; duplicate callbacks and delayed/offline intervals do not replay missed cycles. Warp initiation, docking, gate/GM travel, ship loss and disconnect release control at the saved drone position. Downtime also releases control, clears targets/velocity and performs no offline combat. Reconnect requires owned supported drones in the current system and control range, with current skills/count/bandwidth checks. No transition teleports drones back into the bay or refunds losses.
