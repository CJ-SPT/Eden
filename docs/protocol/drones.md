## Fleet drone assist and guard, November 7, 2026

The final combat-drone checkpoint implements native **CmdAssist(characterID, droneIDs)** and **CmdGuard(characterID, droneIDs)**. The recipient must be another online non-capsule pilot in the same fleet, system and local grid, within the owner's effective drone-control range when assigned. The selection must belong to the owner's current supported ship and retain its earned skills, count and bandwidth. Assist and guard share the pinned **20-drone** recipient limit across all owners. Reassigning the same flight does not consume extra slots; concurrent requests cannot overbook, and invalidated assignments do not retain reservations.

The launching ship remains controller and the original pilot retains ownership, bonuses, damage attribution and legal responsibility. The recipient supplies neither drone skills nor bandwidth. Assigning a flight snapshots the recipient's outgoing or incoming trigger revision, so earlier actions cannot wake newly assigned drones. Assist follows the next paid offensive module action; guard responds to a new incoming attack. These explicit orders operate with passive drone preferences. Drone damage never becomes an offensive-module trigger, preventing assisted flights from recursively activating other flights. New revisions can redirect an already fighting flight. Owner safety, player presence, live target state and effective control range are checked before delegated combat; an inadmissible action is consumed without attacking, and later changing safety or range does not replay it. A fresh offensive action is required.

Idle delegated drones orbit the recipient's ship and return to it after their enemy disappears. Movement retains the existing mass/agility integration and physical travel time. Sentries remain stationary. The owner's existing shutdown distance and physical recall rules still apply. Direct engage, orbit, recall, abandon and reconnect orders clear delegation without clearing paid shot deadlines or repairing drones. Ordinary recovery, destruction and launch paths also clear the saved assignment. Fleet leave/disband, membership replacement, recipient disconnect/warp/docking, ship/system changes and leaving the existing cooperative-fleet grid cancel delegation before another shot. Cancellation advances the command baseline so it cannot resurrect an old automatic-defense action. New fleet membership tokens distinguish leaving/rejoining and connection replacement from ordinary rank or coordinate updates.

Schema **102** adds assignment mode, recipient pilot/ship, fleet membership tokens and consumed reaction revision to each saved drone, with a recipient-ship index. Resident snapshots and SQL fallback share this state, including fatal settlement. Startup releases control and clears assignments, preserving physical position, damage and paid deadlines without offline combat. Neither delegation nor its latest-trigger state grows with shot history. Warmed assist and guard advance through typed resident inputs; observers do not rebuild fits or open connections.

## Drone delegation combat triggers, November 7, 2026

This prerequisite checkpoint for milestone 3 records the two different events needed by drone delegation. Assist must observe a fleet member's paid offensive-module action; guard must observe an actual incoming attack. Generic player hostility cannot distinguish these: it also contains drone orders and drone damage. Reusing it for assist would allow drone attacks to recursively trigger other assisted flights.

`DroneCombatTriggers` keeps independent immutable outgoing/incoming revisions and the latest entity/time for each ship. Separate paid actions at the same clock instant receive different revisions; an older timestamp cannot replace newer state. Storage is one row per participating player ship, rather than a shot log or a source/target-pair history. No drone command, ownership, range, safety or target admission changes in this checkpoint.

Paid player turrets, missile launches, EWAR attempts and targeted tackle advance the outgoing trigger. Player targets also receive an incoming trigger. NPC direct fire, missile launches and in-range EWAR attempts advance incoming state, including failed in-range jams. Out-of-range NPC attempts do not. Player drone shots advance incoming state only. Missile impacts do not replay the earlier launch event, rejected/cooldown calls do not create events, and warp-core stabilization is not an offensive action. Repeated paid cycles use their owning activation operations.

SQL updates share the existing payment/effect transaction. Resident nonfatal turret, NPC and drone paths update typed state under the world owner without SQL after warming. Cached absence is invalidated by the actual committed trigger row; a rolled-back action cannot publish either direction. Immutable trigger snapshots join the same combat checkpoint generation as payment and damage. Schema **101** creates `WorldDroneCombatTriggers` with ship ownership/cascade lifetime. Startup clears these connection-era triggers alongside released drone control, so downtime never supplies an old delegated attack.

### Rules, combat and lifetime

Launch requires real owned bay contents, an in-space living Brutix, the drone's trained prerequisites, enough effective ship bandwidth and at most **min(Drones level, 5)** controlled drones. Selected quantities split into durable singleton identities; the final selected unit may retain its original stack identity. Repeated/concurrent requests cannot duplicate items or exceed limits. Recall returns those same individual IDs rather than creating replacement stacks. Insufficient bay capacity leaves the drone in space. Losing skills or effective bandwidth releases excess drones without moving or deleting them.

Light/Medium Drone Operation supplies 5% damage per level for its size; Drone Interfacing supplies 10%. A racial specialization supplies 2% only when that drone requires it. Drone Sharpshooting modifies optimal, Drone Navigation modifies chase speed and Drone Durability modifies all three HP capacities by their pinned 5%-per-level bonuses. Control range is 20 km plus 5 km per Drone Avionics level and 3 km per Advanced Drone Avionics level. Manual attack requires a locked live NPC and control-range validation; warping NPCs reject new orders and cancel existing drone attacks; accepted drones then maintain their own chase/orbit and firing intervals. Return-and-orbit cancels that attack. Passive drones wait for orders; aggressive drones may defend against NPCs that fire on their controlling ship after the last explicit order. Focus fire chooses a common attacker; disabled focus fire permits distributing drones across attackers. These target-selection policies are Eden's initial behavior, not a claim about Tranquility's private AI.

Drone motion reuses the existing Carbon-derived `OrbitCourse` and the free-ball mass/agility time constant. It distinguishes chase and orbit speeds in both authoritative movement and client orders. Reference: Carbon Destiny **27ee40b38a563d29054b32f64c3ebc87fabaec1f**, [Ballpark.cpp EvolveOldStyleOrbit](https://github.com/carbonengine/destiny/blob/27ee40b38a563d29054b32f64c3ebc87fabaec1f/src/Ballpark.cpp) and [Thunkers.cpp WriteBallToStream](https://github.com/carbonengine/destiny/blob/27ee40b38a563d29054b32f64c3ebc87fabaec1f/src/Thunkers.cpp). Free-ball Follow/Orbit snapshots retain follow ID/range and explicit mass, velocity, agility and speed fraction. See the [Carbon license](../carbon-license.md).

Turret-style drone shots use catalog tracking, signature and range with Eden's existing accuracy/quality and layered resistance calculations. Saved per-drone deadlines prevent duplicate cycles. NPC damage, hostility, kill history, faction/security changes, bounty/tax and wreck creation share the existing settlement transaction. Competing drones cannot settle a kill twice. Native combat messages and timed effects reach the owner and observers. NPC turrets can retaliate against attacking drones; an initial 25% target-selection opportunity shares the NPC's existing weapon deadline, with real shield/armor/structure damage and permanent drone loss. NPC missiles still target the controlling ship. The resident NPC fast path falls back to the same transactional route while drones are controlled, preventing it from bypassing retaliation.

Schema **85** adds `WorldDrone`, `WorldDroneSettings` and the last NPC weapon target, without granting drones, skills, ISK or health. Position, individual damage, paid deadlines and preferences persist. Ordinary one-second owner updates advance movement/combat; duplicate callbacks and delayed/offline intervals do not replay missed cycles. Warp initiation, docking, gate/GM travel, ship loss and disconnect release control at the saved drone position. Downtime also releases control, clears targets/velocity and performs no offline combat. Reconnect requires owned supported drones in the current system and control range, with current skills/count/bandwidth checks. No transition teleports drones back into the bay or refunds losses.
