## Ice harvesting and public ice fields (November 9, 2026)

All **six static ice tools** in build 3662168 now fit and operate: Ice Harvester I/II/ORE
(16278, 22229, 28752) and Ice Mining Laser I/II/ORE (37450, 37451, 37452).
Native ship-group/type restrictions remain enforced, including barge harvesters and the
Perseverance's explicit laser allowance. Abyssal variants remain unavailable.
Ice tools accept raw group-465 blocks of 1,000 m³; ordinary ore tools reject ice, and ice tools
reject ore, gas and compressed ice. Collection chooses the dedicated ice hold, general mining
hold or cargo, where present. Activation requires space for at least one entire block.

A stopped partial cycle consumes its paid capacitor but extracts no ice, generates no critical
yield and removes no residue. Stopping at the completed deadline settles one completed cycle.
The base yield remains 1,000 m³; Mining/Astrogeology do not increase it. Native Ice Harvesting
skill effects 1190/1191 and applicable hull bonuses reduce duration. The pinned Precision and
Exploitation effects 12755/12757 explicitly select Ice Harvesting as well as Mining, so their
critical chance/bonus modifiers apply to completed ice cycles. Native critical output can add
blocks without removing extra stock, and still requires hold space. This follows the pinned
catalog where the older wiki's unconditional one-block wording differs.

The first location cohort contains **four high-security field layouts in 117 positively listed
ice systems**. Native dungeons 5496–5499 supply Blue Ice (21 rocks), White Glaze (19), Glacial
Mass (20) and Clear Icicle (19). Room-local positions come from the pinned catalog. Eden floors
the native fractional stock values to whole blocks and projects physical radius using its ore
geometry convention, square root of stock volume. These are explicit stock/geometry policies,
not a claim that client dungeon radius is measured retail reserve. Positive system lists come
from the corresponding UniWiki belt pages; Pochven entries are omitted. This is an admitted
subset, not complete low/null/wormhole distribution.

Ice fields appear in the onboard scanner and signal tracker as public anomalies with native
archetype 28. No probes are needed. They use the existing anomaly warp route and local solid,
lockable asteroid contacts; arriving does not create a combat encounter. The combat Agency
directory remains combat-only. Ice fields and gas signatures share saved site/deposit identity
in world version 132, but have separate resident views. Finite stock and depletion survive
restart/checkpoint. After the last block is collected, the site retires atomically and returns
**six hours later** with a fresh scanner identity and placement. Eden creates one field per
admitted system, retains unmined fields across restart without a downtime refill, and does not
expire them on a wall-clock lifetime. No ice-specific NPC waves, mining drones, compression,
boosts, low/null-security layouts or shattered-wormhole fields are supplied by this checkpoint.

## Catalog-wide station production (November 9, 2026)

Personal station manufacturing now uses a shared immutable catalog recipe index instead of the starter hull, Tech I, small-hybrid and selected-module whitelists. Of **4,890 manufacturing definitions**, **4,049** have published blueprint/product types, one positive output, a positive duration, nonempty valid material inputs and valid required skills. Exclusions are **809 unpublished blueprints, 3 unpublished products, 24 invalid output/time definitions, 4 invalid skill definitions and 1 invalid material definition**. This is recipe admission before station restrictions, not a claim that every input can be acquired or every product can be flown/used. Every owned installation independently resolves the same indexed recipe and validates skills, quoted quantities/time/fees, blueprint location/ownership, available inputs, licensed runs and slots. Ordinary packaged outputs follow existing inventory, contracts, trade and production-claim market unlock behavior. Unlocking still occurs on successful claim, not installation or readiness.

The reproducible [production audit](../production-catalog.json) includes all manufacturing definitions and exclusions, source fingerprints, complete recipes and representative pinned metadata. Regenerate from the read-only build-3662168 catalog:

```powershell
python tools/Export-ProductionCatalog.py --catalog "$env:LOCALAPPDATA/Eden/catalog.sqlite"
```

Facility product filters and authoritative installation checks share typed station restrictions. Titans and supercarriers cannot be built in NPC stations. Carriers, dreadnoughts (including lancers), force auxiliaries, capital industrial ships and command carriers require low/null security; missing security rejects these capital jobs. The native security boundary is raw **0.45**, consistent with displayed high-security classification. Freighters, jump freighters and ordinary industrial command hulls retain high-security manufacturing. Originals use the client's ceil-of-30-days run limit; manufacturing copies use the lesser of licensed remaining runs and the hard cap, matching the pinned `Manufacturing.job_max_runs` override. The server retains checked output/inventory integer limits.

The Industry blueprint browser and individual reads now include ancient relics (category **34**). Native `industryCommon.BlueprintInstance` marks relics as non-original independently of the physical quantity sentinel; replies retain the actual stack quantity and expose it as available attempts. Invention reserves the relic stack through the existing installed-job guard, consumes catalog datacores/decryptors and skills, and saves selected output, probability, output ME/TE/runs and individual success outcomes once. Finish or cancellation consumes one physical relic per attempt, preserves unreserved units, removes exhausted stacks, releases the science slot and cannot reroll or duplicate output. Installed relics cannot move, merge, enter contracts or be trashed; Stack All leaves them untouched. Blueprint copies retain their separate licensed-run accounting.

World version **130** adds missing positive economic reference prices for valid manufacturing inputs while preserving every existing saved price override and service-cost policy. Defaults use the pinned catalog base price where positive, then the existing dated November-8 market snapshot's sell quote. These are explicit Eden installation-valuation defaults, not imported live adjusted-price claims. The same saved prices are sent to the native price adapter and used for component costs. This backfill creates no items, NPC offers, wallet credits or finished products. Priced science valuations now reject missing material prices rather than treating an absent component as free. Historical insurance policy and hull-admission scopes are unchanged.

Material-source gaps intentionally remain unobtainable by maintainer request. This milestone introduces no moon, gas, ice, planetary, wormhole, exploration-reward or alternate NPC input supply. Jobs remain personal and station-local; corporation jobs, remote jobs, reaction facilities and supercapital infrastructure are separate unimplemented systems. Manufacture does not grant assembly, fitting, flight or structure deployment support to its product. These boundaries prevent a broader recipe list from implying a complete acquisition or operational chain.

### Resource lifecycle

`IResourceRenewal` restores catalog-backed belts independently of connected pilots or system visits. Existing and newly generated belts receive one saved deadline per belt. The host worker checks once per minute, processes at most 32 due belts per admitted batch and serializes batches with market maintenance. Startup drains overdue batches before opening listeners. Restoring a belt and moving its deadline commit together. Restart/repeated calls cannot replay the same boundary; a long absence restores one deposit capacity and schedules the next future boundary, rather than accumulating a stockpile for each missed day.

Renewal reuses the deterministic seeded field: catalog belt/type/count definitions, stable rock IDs, and the existing explicitly local 5,000-15,000 cubic metre deposit policy. Mined or partially depleted generated rocks return to that target. Existing positions, radii and quantities above the generated target are retained. Reconnecting or reading the field does not reset saved quantities. Resident asteroid views invalidate after committed changes, and the existing flight refresh streams restored rocks. Both pilot and faction-NPC miners consume those same saved rows. Unsupported synthetic/developer deposits without a catalog belt are not regenerated by this mechanism. Ice/gas/anomalies and global resource redistribution remain separate work.

CCP's [No Downtime discussion](https://forums.eveonline.com/t/no-downtime-vol-2-feedback-thread/326919?page=3) describes replenishment of ordinary belts as a downtime database activity. Exact resource allocation is unavailable in the client/catalog. Eden's daily full-capacity policy is explicit local configuration, not an implementation claim about Tranquility's undisclosed regeneration algorithm. Renewal continues with wall time while the host is stopped; no NPC activities or earned rewards are replayed for that absence.
